Numwit — Don't guess the number. Guess everyone else.

Numwit is a free real-time multiplayer mind game for 3–8 players. Everyone picks a number from 0 to 100 at the same time. The target is 80% of the average, and the closest pick wins the round. Five penalty points and you're out — last mind standing wins.

Play public rooms, private rooms with friends, or practice against bots. No signup, no download.
